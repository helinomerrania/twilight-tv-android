      YouTube picks the quality itself. To make it use the highest, choose it once in YouTube's own menu on the TV. YouTube then remembers it on that TV for weeks.

      1. On the TV, a pointer appears. The remote's arrow buttons move it and OK clicks. A USB mouse works too.
      2. Move it over the video, so YouTube shows its controls at the bottom. If they don't appear, click the video once.
      3. Click the gear ⚙ at the bottom right (or top right), then Quality, then the highest number (2160p on a 4K TV).
